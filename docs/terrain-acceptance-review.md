# Global terrain acceptance review

## Findings

- **Linear mountain bands:** `planetTerrain.ts` places Gaussian ridge profiles around authored polylines in `PLANET_TERRAIN_CONTROLS.mountainSystems`. The earlier profile used one uniform core and a broad shoulder, so a straight control segment produced parallel, stripe-like relief. Generator v3 adds deterministic low-frequency axis warp, variable width, along-range modulation, and a weaker foreland shoulder. These are still generalized global axes, not surveyed ridges.
- **Stepped terrain classes:** `classify()` applies categorical thresholds (for example, hills at slope `> 4.5°`, mountains at elevation `> 1450 m` with slope/relief conditions, and plateaus at authored weight `> 0.55` with slope `< 10°`). The preview samples a 1° class cell, so the boundary is necessarily quantized. A cell represents about 111 km north-south and up to 111 km east-west at the equator (less east-west toward the poles). This is a data-resolution limit; class edges are not blurred because that would imply unsupported detail.
- **False relief outside the map:** `inverseEqualEarth()` previously returned longitudes beyond ±180° for canvas points outside the projected globe, and the renderer colored the resulting `noData` value as deep ocean. For the 1600×800 projection canvas, the left and right edge at `(x=0 or 1600, y=400)` inverted to roughly `−198°` and `+198°`. The inverse now rejects out-of-domain longitude, and the renderer leaves those pixels transparent.
- **Antimeridian proximity errors:** line segments previously wrapped each endpoint independently. An eastern shelf segment around 145–169°E could therefore be folded through the western edge: near longitude `−11.5°`, latitude `42.5°N`, it falsely appeared about 130 km away, despite its real distance being over 10,000 km. Segment endpoints are now unwrapped relative to one another, preserving their short authored path. The coast-distance seed scan also checks the first and last longitude columns as neighbors.
- **Abrupt polygon transitions:** `regionWeight()` formerly gave near-full effect just outside a control polygon and zero effect just inside its edge. At `−11.5°, 42.5°` and `−10.5°, 42.5°`, that caused the west-shelf DEM to jump from `−2044 m` to `−4388 m`. A signed-distance transition now fades continuously across the boundary; the same samples are `−3348 m` and `−3188 m` after correction.
- **Ocean basins:** the previous ocean model used a global `−4550 m` base, broad periodic noise, and ridge/trench lines, but had no authored basin regions. Four large basin controls now add geographically placed depth variation. Their representative interpolated heights are: western `−5119.5 m` at `[-158,17]`, meridian `−5012.25 m` at `[-54,42]`, eastern `−5215.25 m` at `[64,8]`, and southern `−4557.5 m` at `[-65,-68]`. These remain illustrative controls on a 1° synthetic DEM.

## Integrity and known limits

The eight continent features and authored island IDs are unchanged. The existing geometry checks require unique IDs, valid closed rings, nonzero polygon area, valid coordinate ranges, and no authored segment that crosses the antimeridian. Authored island centers are checked to remain offshore from all continents. Coastline masks still determine land versus ocean, and elevation keeps positive land and negative water values. A maximum remaining neighboring land/sea height difference is expected at steep mountainous coasts; it is not an offshore polygon overlap.

The largest remaining same-surface adjacent DEM step is **2020 m** at approximately `−75.5°, 23.5°S` (3421 m to 1401 m, high mountains to hills). The largest land/sea step is **7607 m** near `133.5°E, 51.5°N` (−3214 m ocean to 4393 m land), where the authored Veyra collision belt reaches the coast. The former within-surface test limit was 3500 m; the measured maximum is below it. These values are broad synthetic terrain signals, not locally accurate heights.

## Preview

Open `/` with `?geography-preview=phase1` and choose **Height**, **Slope**, **Terrain class**, **Shaded relief**, or **Bathymetry**. Use **Reset view** to restore the global frame. The preview reports the current 1° sample spacing. Continental and regional DEM levels remain unavailable.
